### Cross-cutting themes
- Hand-rolled glass instead of the sanctioned primitive: raw .ultraThinMaterial + manual stroke-on-glass, black backing plates, glass-on-glass nesting, and the deprecated liquidGlass shim recur across 9 surfaces (PlanetInfoCard, SpatialRevealCard, CategoryRail, SearchDock transcript, OnboardingOverlay, AddToolSheet bar, RootSheet, AccountSettingsSheet, every ToolDetailSection card). This is the single biggest systemic defect and the source of most P1s.
- Accent/category color used far above the =<0.12 tint cap as fills, tints, strokes and colored glows on 9 surfaces (category pill full-opacity, 0.45 strokes, 0.4 tints, 0.16-0.20 badges, colored drop-shadows) when accent should live only on label/glyph/status-dot.
- Off-grid magic spacing (6/7/9/11/13/14/15/18/22/28) used instead of BrandSpacing tokens is the dominant volume theme and the root of every user-reported 'uneven/awkward rhythm' complaint.
- Fixed .system(size:) fonts bypass BrandTypography across ~8 surfaces + the toast/button primitives, losing both .rounded family and Dynamic Type.
- Bespoke eyebrows (caption/bold + tracking 1.1/1.2/0.7) reimplemented on 6 surfaces instead of the one-line .brandEyebrow() (caption2/semibold/kerning 1.8/uppercase).
- Sub-44pt touch targets (28/30/34/36/38/40) recur because the LiquidGlassButton primitive never floors the hit area and callers don't add .hitArea().
- Inline .white.opacity()/Color.red for text, fills and strokes instead of BrandColor tokens is pervasive (CategoryRail, BranchChip, SearchDock, Onboarding, AccountSettingsSheet, ChatScreen/ChatTheme, ToolDetailSection).
- Chip chrome is reimplemented by hand in feature code and three chip paddings coexist on the same surfaces (11/7 actionChip vs 10/6 ToolChip vs 10/8 CategoryRail), plus bespoke Experimental/category/missing-tool capsules.
- Press scale is inconsistent across the button/chip family: 0.93 (CategoryRail), 0.96 (ToolChip/AddTool/GlassMorphCluster), 0.97 (sanctioned/LiquidGlassButton), 0.98 (AccountSettings rows).
- The primary-action recipe (accent fill + white 0.18 strokeStrong overlay + accent shadow) is applied only partially, so Send vs +, onboarding CTA, and tool-detail primary vs copy visibly disagree.
- 'Between major sections' gaps use xl=20 where BrandSpacing.section=32 is the intended token, leaving AddTool/Account/ToolDetail/Chat sections cramped.
